[r24] ITEM: Applicable Sample Dimension Range
      desc: The range of sample sizes compatible with this protocol at the target voxel size and geometric configuration. The upper bound is a hard constraint set by the instrument field of view (FOV) at the protocol's target magnification: FOV ≈ detector array width × pixel size / geometric magnification (divide additionally by optical objective multiplier for Versa-class systems using optical magnification). For Mode A (single-volume): all three sample dimensions must fall within the FOV. For Mode B (multi-volume stitching): sample diameter must fall within the FOV; sample length along the rotation axis is effectively unlimited through stitching. The lower bound is a practical guideline: samples spanning fewer than ~10 voxels in their smallest dimension are difficult to characterize reliably.
      Ptier='Basic'  Atier='Read-Only'
      dtype: Text (free)
      allowed: e.g., 'Single-volume: max 20 mm in all dimensions at 7.6 µm voxel' | 'Multi-volume stitching: max 25 mm diameter, unlimited length at 12.9 µm voxel' | 'Max 5 mm (all axes) at 0.85 µm voxel with 4× objective'
      modes: ['Single-volume', 'Multi-volume stitching']

[r46] ITEM: Accelerating Voltage
      desc: X-ray tube accelerating voltage in kilovolts (kV) registered by the protocol. Determines the maximum X-ray photon energy and controls penetration depth and phase contrast. Higher voltages provide greater penetration for dense or large samples; lower voltages improve contrast between low-density phases. If the voltage was varied across samples within the same protocol or session, report the full range applied (e.g., 90–115 kV). Note: some sources report this parameter as 'X keV' (maximum Bremsstrahlung photon energy) rather than 'X kV' (tube voltage). For polychromatic lab XCT, these are numerically equivalent: E_max [keV] = V [kV]. Record the value as stated in the source and add a parenthetical note if the unit used is keV.
      Ptier='Basic'  Atier='Editable'
      dtype: Numeric (kV)
      allowed: e.g., 80 kV | 130 kV | 160 kV | 190 kV; for variable settings: 90–115 kV
      modes: ['Single-volume', 'Multi-volume stitching']

[r47] ITEM: Tube Current
      desc: X-ray tube current in microamperes (µA) registered by the protocol. Controls photon flux and therefore projection image SNR. Higher current improves SNR but increases thermal loading on the source and may increase focal spot size. If the current was varied across samples within the same protocol or session, report the full range applied (e.g., 65–115 µA).
      Ptier='Basic'  Atier='Editable'
      dtype: Numeric (µA)
      allowed: e.g., 89 µA | 108 µA | 180 µA; for variable settings: 65–115 µA
      modes: ['Single-volume', 'Multi-volume stitching']

[r48] ITEM: X-ray Power
      desc: X-ray tube power in watts (W) registered by the protocol. Derivable as voltage (kV) × current (mA) = kV × µA / 1000. Power constrains the achievable focal spot size for microfocal tubes: higher power requires a larger focal spot, trading spatial resolution for SNR. Retained as a standalone field because it is routinely reported in the literature and directly constrains instrument operating limits. If power was varied across samples within the session, report the full range applied (e.g., 7–13 W).
      Ptier='Advanced'  Atier='Editable'
      dtype: Numeric (W)
      allowed: e.g., 9 W | 14 W | 25 W; for variable settings: 7–13 W
      modes: ['Single-volume', 'Multi-volume stitching']

Instrument-proprietary filter codes should be decoded where possible.
      Ptier='Basic'  Atier='Editable'
      dtype: Text (free)

      Ptier='Advanced'  Atier='Editable'

[r83] ITEM: Spatial Resolution
      desc: Effective spatial resolution of the reconstructed CT volume, which typically differs from (and is coarser than) the voxel size due to the detector point spread function (PSF), geometric unsharpness, and the reconstruction filter.
